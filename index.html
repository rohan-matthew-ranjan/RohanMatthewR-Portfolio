What I do

Most business problems arrive as a pile of opinions. My work is to find the real question underneath, split it into pieces someone can act on, and line up competing priorities into one decision framework.

I started in mechanical engineering, moved into business analysis at a software studio in Chennai, then completed an MSc in Limerick and joined The Shannon Airport Group as a management associate. The thread throughout: clear thinking, honest numbers, and communication senior leaders can use.
